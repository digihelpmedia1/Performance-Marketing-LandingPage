Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to what business owners usually ask before starting. Have a question that isn't here? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much budget do I need to start running ads?

There's no single right number. It depends on your industry, location, what a customer is worth to you, and how competitive your market is. Most businesses start with a modest test budget to find out what a qualified lead actually costs, then scale what works. On the strategy call we'll recommend a starting budget that fits your goals, and we'll tell you honestly if we think the budget is too small to give reliable results.

How soon can I expect results from Meta Ads or Google Ads?

Campaigns can start bringing in traffic and inquiries within days of launch, but the first few weeks are a learning phase where the platforms and our team test audiences, keywords and creatives. Stable, improving performance usually takes a few weeks to a couple of months of data and optimization. We can't guarantee specific results, but we'll show you clearly what's happening and what we're improving at every stage.

Which is better for my business: Meta Ads or Google Ads?

They do different jobs. Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for what you offer, which is ideal when demand exists. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) reaches people based on their interests and behaviour, which is ideal for creating demand, visual offers and retargeting. Many businesses benefit from both, but not always at the start. We'll recommend the right mix after understanding your business.

Do you provide both advertising strategy and campaign management?

Yes. We handle the full process: research and strategy, campaign setup, ad creative direction, tracking, day-to-day management, optimization and reporting. You stay informed and in control, without having to manage the technical work yourself.

Can you help improve the quality of my leads?

Improving lead quality is one of our main priorities. We work on targeting, ad messaging, qualifying questions in lead forms, landing page clarity and conversion tracking, so the platforms learn to find more people like your best customers. We also ask for your feedback on lead quality, because your sales conversations are the best signal we have.

Do you provide landing page optimization?

Yes. Ads only bring people to the door; the landing page decides whether they inquire. We review your existing pages and recommend or implement improvements to headlines, offers, forms, trust elements and page speed, so more of your paid traffic turns into inquiries.

Do I need an existing website to run advertising campaigns?

Not necessarily. Meta lead-form campaigns and WhatsApp or call-focused campaigns can work without a website. That said, a focused landing page usually gives you better control over lead quality and tracking. If you don't have one, we'll advise on the simplest way to get started.
