Last updated: November 2026

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing your information

We only share your information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, form handling, email and CRM tools), and only as needed to provide our services, or where required by law.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes above and take reasonable measures to protect it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've shared with us at any time by contacting us.
